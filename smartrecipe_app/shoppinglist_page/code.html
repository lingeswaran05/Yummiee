Vegetable Biryani

A fragrant and flavorful Indian rice dish packed with mixed vegetables, warm spices, and aromatic basmati rice. Perfect for a comforting dinner.

Servings
2

Ingredients

shopping_cartGo to Shopping List
  • 1 cup Basmati Rice
  • 2 cups Mixed Vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
  • 1 tbsp Biryani Masala

Instructions

  1. 1

    Wash and soak 1 cup of Basmati rice for 30 minutes. Drain completely and set aside. In a large pot, bring water to a boil with whole spices (cloves, cardamom, cinnamon) and cook rice until 70% done.

  2. 2

    Heat oil or ghee in a large heavy-bottomed pan. Add cumin seeds, sliced onions, and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté until onions are golden brown.

  3. 3

    Add the mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans, cauliflower) along with Biryani Masala, turmeric, and chili powder. Cook for 5-7 minutes until the vegetables are slightly tender.

  4. 4

    Layer the partially cooked rice over the vegetable base. Sprinkle with saffron-infused milk, fresh mint, and fried onions.

  5. 5

    Cover tightly with a lid (or seal with dough for 'Dum' style). Cook on very low heat for 15-20 minutes until the rice is fully cooked and fragrant.
